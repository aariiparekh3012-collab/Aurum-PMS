Compliance & Onboarding

📋

Compliance review queue

Portfolio Management

💼

Portfolio construction

Assign strategies (LCV, MCG, FCC), build equity holdings, manage cash ledger positions, and track inception-to-date portfolio composition.

📈

Trading desk

Place and track equity orders with order-book visibility. Pending, filled, and cancelled states with broker assignments and settlement dates.

Performance reporting

90-day NAV history with donut AUM breakdown, period returns (1M / 3M / 6M / 1Y / SI), and benchmark comparison — all rendered as live charts.

Workflow

From application to statement

A guided, end-to-end process that your compliance and RM teams can navigate without training.

1

Onboard a new applicant

RM fills the multi-step KYC form — personal details, risk questionnaire, bank account, demat BO ID, and nominees. Application is submitted for compliance review.

2

Compliance reviews & approves

Compliance officer reviews the full application in their queue. One click approves the applicant, converting them to an active client and unlocking portfolio setup.

3

Portfolio is constructed

A portfolio account is opened, strategy assigned (LCV / MCG / FCC), equity holdings entered, and opening cash position recorded.

4

Trading desk executes orders

Orders are placed and tracked through the trading module — pending orders visible to compliance, filled trades logged with broker and settlement details.

5

Performance is tracked & reported

Daily NAV snapshots accumulate into charts and period returns. Client and RM can download a PDF statement at any time.

Common Questions

Got questions? We have answers

Which brokers does Aurum integrate with?

›

Aurum integrates with major Indian brokers including NSE, BSE, and NSDL. Custom broker integrations are available for enterprise deployments. Contact our team for specific broker requirements.

Is Aurum SEBI-compliant?

›

Yes. Aurum is built from the ground up to comply with SEBI regulations for PMS operators. The onboarding flow captures all required KYC data, maintains comprehensive audit trails, and ensures investor accreditation validation.

How long does deployment take?

›

Production deployment typically takes 1–2 weeks for standard setups. Docker Compose enables one-command infrastructure spin-up. A dedicated onboarding specialist guides you through data migration and user training.

Can we self-host or use our VPC?

›

Yes. Aurum supports self-hosted deployments on your infrastructure or a dedicated VPC. We also offer managed hosting. Choose the option that best fits your security and compliance posture.

What's the onboarding support like?

›

Every deployment includes a dedicated onboarding specialist, 2 full days of team training, data migration assistance, and 30-day post-launch support. Enterprise plans include ongoing strategic guidance.

Is there an API for third-party integrations?

›

Yes. Aurum exposes a comprehensive REST API documented in OpenAPI format. You can integrate with any third-party system — accounting, CRM, MIS tools, etc. Full API docs are included in every deployment.
